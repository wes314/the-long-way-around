There is usually a faster way, but is it always better? That depends what you are trying to achieve.

This is where **The Long Way Around** comes in.

In engineering, it means taking the route where you learn the underlying principles rather than simply finding the quickest solution.

In life, sometimes the slower, harder path is the one where you discover something unexpected.

For some people, their satisfaction comes from learning, curiosity and understanding how things work.

This is a record of my explorations in electronics, computers and machines.

My hope is that some of these experiments will be useful to others, or perhaps inspire someone else to take their own long way around.
